         QUADCOPTER FRAME
 So !! after combining all of my CAD knowledge i came up with this Quadcopter frame lol !
 Its basically a frame for your custom quadcopter, it has 4 ends to put a motor with prop also it has cuttings and holes in the center for central hub where u an put your controller, battery and stuff with help of screws or zippers, extra space and holes are provided! !

 Firstly i drew the sketch for it, thru mirroring and stuff tool when i was ready with the sketch i extruded the parts accordingly  !! that's it guys !! Check it out!
